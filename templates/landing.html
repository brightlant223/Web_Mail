Getting access

How to log in

No registration — your admin sets up your mailbox. Three quick steps and you're in.

1

Get your credentials

Your admin creates your mailbox and gives you an email & password. There's no self sign-up.

2

Open the login page

Tap Login above, enter your company email — like yourname@brightlant.com — and your password.

3

You're in

Land in your inbox, turn on notifications, and start sending. Forgot your password? Use the link on the login page.

Install Brightlant as an app

Get one-tap access from your home screen or desktop — no app store needed. It runs full-screen and loads instantly.

On iPhone/iPad: tap Share, then “Add to Home Screen.” On desktop Chrome/Edge: use the install icon in the address bar.
